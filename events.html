Pilot Project Venue Information

Location: Ethiopia, Addis Ababa

Exact venue: To be announced shortly

The ETSA Shootout Series will be hosted in Addis Ababa, bringing together some of the Africa’s most promising esports talents. The event will run on iRacing using the FIA F4, ensuring a professional and globally recognized competitive standard.

Shootout Format

The ETSA Ethiopia Shootout is designed to identify the most disciplined and high‑potential drivers across the country as a Pilot Project. The structure is as follows:

• 3‑Day Shootout: All registered and members of the Ethiopian Motor Sport Association participants compete to qualify for the final round.
• 2‑Day Final Qualifying: The top performers battle for the final ranking.
• Top 5 Selection: The five best contenders will qualify for the upcoming African Esport Championship.
